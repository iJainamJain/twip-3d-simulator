How to play

              
    
                
  • Drive with WASD / arrow keys or the drive stick; the chase cam follows you.
    • 
                
  • On a phone or tablet: steer with the stick under your left thumb, drag the view with another finger to look around, pinch to zoom.
    • 
                
  • Follow the amber light beam, the arrow at the top of the view and the minimap.
    • 
                
  • Each fall adds +3 s. Self-Right stands you back up; Reset Pose respawns at the last checkpoint.
    • 
                
  • Hitting walls or boulders at speed can knock the robot over.
    • 
              

            


            

              
Terrain guide

              

                
Mountains: too steep, wheels slide back down

                
Hills: climbable, lean into them

                
Frozen pond & black ice: almost no grip

                
Mud & gravel: drag slows you down

                
Boulders & walls: solid
